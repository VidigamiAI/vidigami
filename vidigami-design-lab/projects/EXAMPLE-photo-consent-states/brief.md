# EXAMPLE — Photo consent states: Design brief

> Sample brief to show the format. Replace with real projects via `/new-project <JIRA-KEY>`.

## Problem
Staff can't tell at a glance when a tagged student has photo consent withheld, risking
accidental sharing with families.

## Users and scenarios
- Teacher reviewing photos after an event, on a phone
- Communications manager choosing photos for the newsletter, on desktop

## Requirements
| # | Requirement | Priority | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Restricted students are visibly marked on any photo they're tagged in | Must | Example |
| 2 | The marker doesn't rely on color alone | Must | WCAG 1.4.1 |
| 3 | Staff understand what the restriction means for sharing | Should | Example |

## Success criteria
A teacher persona can identify every restricted photo in the set without help.

## Open questions
- [ ] Should staff see *why* consent is withheld? (Likely no — privacy.)
